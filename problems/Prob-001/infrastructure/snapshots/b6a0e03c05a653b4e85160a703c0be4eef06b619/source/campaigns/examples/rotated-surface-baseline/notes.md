# Rotated Surface Baseline

This example campaign is intentionally tiny.

- one committed candidate
- one real `rsinter` benchmark task contract
- three real `rsinter` decoder contracts: `rmatching`, `rbposd`, and `rilpqec`
- one committed rotated-d3 golden fixture for preflight

Its purpose is to lock down the M1 search-layer contracts for issue `#3` and issue `#8`.
